Biography

Parthasarathy Ghose

Parthasarathy Ghose is a theoretical physicist, historian and philosopher of science whose research career spans more than six decades. Trained under National Professor Satyendra Nath Bose, he has made contributions to particle physics, quantum field theory, foundations of quantum mechanics, Bohmian mechanics, stochastic approaches to quantum theory, classical entanglement, quantum cognition, neural dynamics, and philosophy of science.

His work has appeared in Physical Review, Physical Review A and D, Physics Letters A and B, Foundations of Physics, Pramana, Physica A, Computational and Structural Biotechnology Journal and Physica Scripta.

He is the author or editor of numerous books, including Testing Quantum Mechanics on New Ground, Einstein, Tagore and the Nature of Reality, and An Interdisciplinary Approach to Cognitive Modelling.
