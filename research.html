Research

Seven threads, one inquiry

From particle physics and quantum foundations to cognition and consciousness.

The earlier contributions to particle physics and quantum foundations sit alongside more recent work on stochastic mechanics, quantum cognition and neural dynamics. They are connected by a single question: what is the relation between the classical and the quantum, and between matter and mind?

01

Particle Physics and Quantum Field Theory

Early and mid-career research on broken SU(3) symmetry, vector meson dominance, gauge theories, grand unification, Higgs mass corrections, QCD sum rules, quark confinement and quark–gluon plasma physics.

02

Foundations of Quantum Mechanics

Contributions to wave–particle duality, complementarity, EPR correlations, Bohmian mechanics, the relativistic quantum mechanics of bosons, spontaneous collapse, and the quantum–classical transition.

03

Classical Entanglement and Contextuality

Among the early contributors to Hilbert-space formulations of classical optics and to studies of entanglement-like phenomena in classical electromagnetic fields.

04

Stochastic Mechanics and the Quantum–Classical Interface

Current work on Nelson stochastic mechanics, quantum–classical interpolation and persistent stochastic electrodynamics, building on the earlier papers on the continuous transition between quantum and classical mechanics.

05

Quantum Cognition and Neural Dynamics

Interdisciplinary frameworks connecting cognition, probability theory, philosophy and quantum-inspired decision models; recent work on stochastic and quantum-like markers in neural dynamics, including quantum noise in neuronal models and temporal quantum correlations.

06

Cosmology

Recent investigations of Hamiltonian monodromy and quantum spectra in cosmological minisuperspace models.

07

History and Philosophy of Science; Mind, Brain and Consciousness

Writings on the history of modern physics, on S. N. Bose and Einstein, on Tagore and the nature of reality, and on the philosophy of science, now extended to mind–brain–consciousness studies at IIT Mandi.
